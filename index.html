    Funnier in person

    Anthony Fuentes is a stand-up comedian with sharp storytelling and a stage presence that wins over every room he walks into. His act runs on family chaos, cultural commentary and the everyday absurd.

    Online, his videos have pulled millions of views and built a following of more than a million people on Facebook alone. In the clubs, he pairs quick punchlines with an easy charisma, headlining rooms like Zanies, the Improv, Funny Bone, Laugh Factory and Hyena's.

    The people who find him on their phones keep showing up to the clubs, and they leave saying the same thing: he's even funnier live.
